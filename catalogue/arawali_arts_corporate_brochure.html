Manufacturing Capabilities & Timber Engineering

Industrial Kiln Seasoning • Wrought Iron Jaali Fabrication

Equilibrium Kiln Seasoning (8%–12%)

Every log of Sheesham (Dalbergia sissoo), Mango (Mangifera indica), and Acacia undergoes chemical seasoning in our automated hot-air kilns, guaranteeing dimensional stability and zero splitting under dry central heating.

Architectural Ironmongery & Jaali

Our in-house blacksmithing forge pairs authentic hand-pierced Rajasthani jaali lattices with multi-stage electrostatic anti-rust powder coatings, built for humid coastal and sub-zero climates.
