✦ LEGAL & COMMERCIAL GOVERNANCE ✦

Protective Terms Embedded in Every Proforma Invoice (PI)

To guarantee risk symmetry and eliminate post-production disputes, every commercial quote and Proforma Invoice issued by Arawali Arts incorporates five non-negotiable protective clauses:

1. Delivery Terms & Risk Transfer (Incoterms® 2020)

All prices quoted strictly FOB Mundra Port, India. Risk of loss and damage transfers completely to the buyer once the shipping container is placed on board the nominated vessel at Mundra Port (INMUN1) and is loaded on board at Mundra Port. Ocean freight, marine insurance, destination customs clearance, and inland delivery are strictly for the buyer's account.

2. Payment Terms & Bill of Lading (B/L) Control

30% non-refundable advance deposit via Telegraphic Transfer (TT) upon Proforma Invoice confirmation to schedule kiln drying and timber milling. The remaining 70% balance is payable within 5 business days upon presentation of scanned Original Bill of Lading (OBL), commercial invoice, packing list, and compliance certificates. Original Bills of Lading / Telex Release are issued strictly upon receipt of 100% cleared funds.

3. Vessel Nomination & Port Demurrage Liability

Arawali Arts notifies the buyer of cargo readiness 10 business days prior to scheduled factory completion. The buyer and their nominated freight forwarder must secure shipping space and provide container booking details within 14 calendar days of readiness notice. Any ICD or port ground rent, demurrage, or container detention charges resulting from forwarder delay or vessel rollovers shall be borne entirely by the buyer.

4. Nominated Forwarder Local Charges Cap

Arawali Arts pays standard origin Terminal Handling Charges (THC), container haulage, and Bill of Lading issuance at official published port tariffs. If the buyer's nominated shipping agent or NVOCC attempts to impose non-standard CFS, origin carting, or excessive documentation surcharges, Arawali Arts reserves the right to bill these unapproved fees back to the buyer's final account before B/L endorsement.

5. Natural Timber & Handcrafted Artisanal Variance

All solid hardwoods (Sheesham, Mango, Acacia) are seasoned to strict 8%–10% moisture equilibrium. Due to authentic organic handcrafting, natural timber characteristics—including wood grain swirls, live-edge curvature, micro-knots, and hand-hammered wrought iron patina variations—are hallmarks of artisanal authenticity and do not constitute manufacturing defects or grounds for commercial rejection.

Buyer questions

Can I mix SKUs in one container?

Yes. Most buyers ship 25–60 SKUs per 40HQ. We consolidate cartons by SKU, keep a packing list per line, and load to your CBM target.

Do you supply the US Lacey Act and EUDR paperwork?

Yes. Botanical species, harvest origin and supplier declarations are prepared per shipment; VRIKSH legality certificates and ISPM-15 heat treatment are standard.

What is the lead time for a repeat order?

Repeat SKUs usually run 30–40 days. New finishes or carved tooling add 10–14 days for first-article approval.

Can you produce to our drawings?

Yes, for OEM programmes above 100 pcs per SKU. Send a drawing or reference photo and we return a costed counter-sample plan.

How is the container packed?

5-ply export cartons, corner buffers, moisture barrier, ISPM-15 heat-treated dunnage. Carton marks follow your importer instructions.
