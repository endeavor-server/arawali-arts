Buyer questions

Can I mix SKUs in one container?

Yes. Most buyers ship 25–60 SKUs per 40HQ. We consolidate cartons by SKU, keep a packing list per line, and load to your CBM target.

Do you supply the US Lacey Act and EUDR paperwork?

Yes. Botanical species, harvest origin and supplier declarations are prepared per shipment; VRIKSH legality certificates and ISPM-15 heat treatment are standard.

What is the lead time for a repeat order?

Repeat SKUs usually run 30–40 days. New finishes or carved tooling add 10–14 days for first-article approval.

Can you produce to our drawings?

Yes, for OEM programmes above 100 pcs per SKU. Send a drawing or reference photo and we return a costed counter-sample plan.

How is the container packed?

5-ply export cartons, corner buffers, moisture barrier, ISPM-15 heat-treated dunnage. Carton marks follow your importer instructions.
