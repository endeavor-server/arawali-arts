SECTION 01

US Lacey Act & VRIKSH Timber Legality Verification

🇺🇸 USA IMPORT REGULATION

The United States Lacey Act mandates that all commercial timber imports declare scientific genus, botanical species, and legal point of harvest. For our Indian Rosewood (Dalbergia sissoo), Arawali Arts provides chain-of-custody certificates through the VRIKSH Timber Legality Assessment Scheme, governed by the Export Promotion Council for Handicrafts (EPCH) and recognized under CITES Appendix II exemptions.

Operational Compliance Safeguards

  • ✓
    Legal Procurement Verification: Direct provenance documentation from certified state forest auctions and authorized private farmland mandis across Rajasthan and northern India.
  • ✓
    Pre-Lading PPQ-505 Declarations: Complete United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service (APHIS) Form 505 filings validated prior to Bill of Lading issuance.
  • ✓
    CITES-Compliant Clearance: Official CITES Appendix II comparable documentation ensuring zero detention risk at US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) ports of entry.

SECTION 02

European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR)

🇪🇺 EU DIRECTIVE 2023/1115

Under Regulation (EU) 2023/1115, timber and woodcraft imported into the European Union must be proven to originate from land that has not experienced deforestation after December 31, 2020. Arawali Arts tracks all log batches with precise geographic coordinate documentation and provides due diligence statements (DDS) for European retail chains and wholesale distributors.

Operational Compliance Safeguards

  • ✓
    Farm & Agro-Forestry Plot Traceability: Geolocation polygon and point coordinates mapped for every Sheesham, seasoned Mango, and Acacia timber procurement lot.
  • ✓
    EU Due Diligence Reference Keys: Official reference numbers pre-formatted for direct upload to the European Commission Due Diligence Information System (TRACES NT).
  • ✓
    REACH Chemical Directive Certified: Certified non-toxic, food-safe, lead-free stains, lacquers, and wax oils compliant with EU REACH Annex XVII standards.

Scientific Kiln Seasoning (8%–10% Equilibrium Moisture SLA)

⚙️ FACTORY ENGINEERING SLA

The primary failure point of traditional Indian furniture exporters is timber warping, split joints, or glue separation when shipped from desert climates to centrally heated, air-conditioned Western homes. Arawali Arts eliminates this risk through state-of-the-art automated steam-assisted seasoning chambers.

Operational Compliance Safeguards

  • ✓
    15-Day Automated Seasoning Cycle: Microprocessor-controlled steam and heat schedules bringing timber core moisture down to exactly 8%–10% equilibrium.
  • ✓
    Borate Vacuum Pressure Impregnation: Eco-friendly inorganic borate salt treatment deeply penetrating sapwood and heartwood to permanently prevent borer beetles and termites.
  • ✓
    Batch Digital Moisture Testing: Calibrated multi-pin digital resistance probes testing core and surface moisture, recorded on individual batch QC travel tags.
