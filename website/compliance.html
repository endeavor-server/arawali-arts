1. US Lacey Act & VRIKSH Timber Legality Verification

USA COMPLIANCE

The United States Lacey Act mandates that all timber imports declare scientific genus, species, and legal point of harvest. For our Indian Rosewood (*Dalbergia sissoo*), Arawali Arts provides chain-of-custody certificates through the **VRIKSH Timber Legality Assessment Scheme** (governed by EPCH and recognized by CITES Appendix II exemptions).

  • ✓ Legal procurement verification from certified state forest auctions & private farmland mandis
  • ✓ Complete PPQ 505 Lacey Act declaration forms filled and submitted prior to bill of lading issuance
  • ✓ CITES-compliant exemption permits for smooth US Customs (CBP) clearance

2. European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR)

EU DIRECTIVE

Under the EUDR mandate, timber imported into the EU must be proven to originate from land that has not experienced deforestation after December 31, 2020. Arawali Arts tracks timber batches with geographic coordinate documentation and provides due diligence statements (DDS) for European buyers.

  • ✓ Farm-level and plantation plot traceability for Sheesham, Mango, and Acacia logs
  • ✓ Traceable reference numbers for submission into the EU Due Diligence Information System
  • ✓ Certified non-toxic, lead-free lacquers compliant with European REACH chemical directives

3. Scientific Kiln Seasoning (8%–12% Moisture Control)

FACTORY STANDARD

The primary failure point of traditional Indian furniture exporters is timber warping, cracking, or glue separation when shipped from dry desert heat (Jodhpur) to humid or centrally heated western homes. We eliminate this with industrial kiln chambers:

  • ✓ 15-day steam-assisted kiln seasoning cycle bringing timber moisture down to exactly 8%–12%
  • ✓ Chemical pressure impregnation with eco-friendly borate salts protecting against termites and powder-post beetles
  • ✓ Calibrated pin-type digital moisture testing recorded on individual quality control tags

4. ISPM-15 Wood Packaging & ISTA-3A Drop-Testing

LOGISTICS & TRANSIT

Zero transit breakage is our standard. Our packaging engineering meets parcel transit standards (FedEx / UPS / DHL) and container ocean freight:

  • ✓ All solid timber pallets, wooden crates, and dunnage carry official IPPC heat-treatment (HT) stamps
  • ✓ 5-ply and 7-ply heavy-duty corrugated cartons with 25mm high-density expanded polyethylene (EPE) corner protectors
  • ✓ Silica gel moisture-trap desiccant bags (250g per pallet) placed inside sea containers to eliminate sweat condensation
