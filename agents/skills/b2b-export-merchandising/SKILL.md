# B2B Export Merchandising Skill

## Purpose
Guides agents in translating raw product data, artisan dimensions, and factory costs into professional B2B export marketing collateral and quotation systems.

## Core Formulae & Standards
1. **CBM (Cubic Meter) Calculation**:
   $$\text{CBM} = \frac{\text{Length (cm)} \times \text{Width (cm)} \times \text{Height (cm)}}{1,000,000}$$
2. **Container Loading Baselines**:
   * **20ft General Purpose (FCL)**: ~28–30 usable CBM (~18,000 kg payload)
   * **40ft High Cube (HQ)**: ~68–70 usable CBM (~26,000 kg payload)
   * **LCL (Less than Container Load)**: Minimum 3–5 CBM on heat-treated wooden pallets.
3. **Incoterms Definition**:
   * **FOB Mundra / Kandla**: Seller covers transport to vessel port in Gujarat; buyer covers ocean freight and destination customs.
   * **CIF (Destination Port)**: Seller covers ocean freight and marine insurance.
   * **DDP**: Used primarily for e-commerce air shipments or Amazon FBA prep.

## Line Sheet Structure
Every export line sheet generated must include:
* **Item Code (SKU)**
* **High-Resolution Photography**
* **Wood Species & Metal Treatment**
* **Product Dimensions (Inches + Centimeters)**
* **Carton Pack Dimensions & CBM**
* **Units per Master Carton**
* **MOQ (Units or Sets)**
* **FOB Port Price (USD)**
