# Jodhpur Furniture & Handicraft Competitor Intelligence Skill

## Purpose
Enables autonomous agents to monitor competitor catalogs, identify new product launches, analyze material selections (Sheesham, Acacia, Mango, Iron, Cane, Bone Inlay), and benchmark Arawali Arts' export positioning against established Jodhpur export houses.

## Competitor Monitoring Registry
Track the 9 key Jodhpur exporters:
1. **Bothra International**: Benchmark for industrial/vintage case goods and global certification (VRIKSH, REACH, C-TPAT).
2. **Prince Art Export**: Benchmark for antique reproductions, carved furniture, and bone inlay.
3. **Aztec Shiva**: Benchmark for case goods, upholstered chairs, and High Point Market trends.
4. **Latiyal Handicrafts**: Benchmark for reclaimed industrial, cast iron furniture, and US big-box accounts (Crate & Barrel).
5. **Sohan Handicrafts**: Benchmark for carved solid wood, nesting tables, and ethnic mirrors.
6. **Woodpecker Living**: Benchmark for Scandinavian-Jodhpur modern, Japandi, and solid wood tabletop.
7. **Lohiya Exports**: Benchmark for wrought-iron decor, wall sconces, and rustic accents.
8. **Neelkanth Art & Craft**: Benchmark for cane-wood fusion and modern bedroom suites.
9. **Thar Export**: Benchmark for heavy-gauge metal-wood commercial/restaurant furniture.

## Standard Competitor Extraction Procedure
When inspecting competitor catalogs:
1. **Extract Categories**: Tabletop, Living, Dining, Bedroom, Wall Decor, Specialty.
2. **Document Dimensions & Materials**: Note wood species, iron gauge, hardware types, and finishes (honey, walnut, burnt charcoal, whitewash).
3. **Analyze MOQ & Packaging**: Check if the competitor offers LCL mixed container loading or mandates 40ft single-SKU FCL.
4. **Identify Product Gaps**: Highlight items Arawali Arts can manufacture at faster lead times or lower MOQs.
5. **Isolate Data**: Always store intelligence in `/Users/endeavoracademy/OpenBot/Shared/Projects/ArawaliArts/research/`. Never store or index in Cognee or Obsidian.
