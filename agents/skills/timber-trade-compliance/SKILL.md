# International Timber Trade Compliance & Quality Control Skill

## Purpose
Enforces regulatory compliance across all wood and iron shipments from Jodhpur to prevent customs rejections, impoundments, or fines in the United States and the European Union.

## Regulatory Protocols
1. **US Lacey Act**:
   * Requires formal declaration of scientific genus and species (e.g., *Dalbergia sissoo* for Sheesham, *Mangifera indica* for Mango wood, *Acacia nilotica* for Acacia).
   * Country of harvest documentation.
2. **VRIKSH Scheme (EPCH India)**:
   * Mandatory verification scheme recognized internationally for Indian Rosewood (Sheesham) under CITES Appendix II exemptions.
   * Chain of custody certification from forest auction / timber mandi to finished export unit.
3. **EUDR (EU Deforestation Regulation)**:
   * Mandates geolocation polygons for harvest origins.
   * Due Diligence Statement (DDS) submitted via EU portal prior to import clearance.
4. **Moisture Control & Kiln Drying**:
   * Moisture content must read **8% to 12%** using calibrated pin-type moisture meters before packaging.
   * Avoid shipment in high monsoon periods without silica gel desiccants (250g per container corner).
5. **ISPM-15 Wood Packaging**:
   * All wooden pallets, crates, and dunnage must carry the official IPPC heat-treatment stamp (HT) or Methyl Bromide fumigation stamp (MB).
