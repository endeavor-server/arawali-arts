## 3. Materials, Production & Jodhpur Finishing Trends
### A. Hardwood Selection
* **Sheesham (Indian Rosewood - *Dalbergia sissoo*)**:
  - Characteristics: Dense grain, rich contrasting dark & light heartwood, luxury feel.
  - Ideal for: Keepsake boxes, luxury coasters, tea light frames, statement dining tables.
  - Requirement: VRIKSH / CITES documentation for international clearance.
* **Mango Wood (*Mangifera indica*)**:
  - Characteristics: Highly sustainable (fruit-bearing byproduct), receptive to diverse stains (bleached, walnut, whitewash, charred black).
  - Trending in: US & European Scandinavian and Japandi decor lines.
* **Acacia Wood**:
  - Characteristics: Water-resistant, hard, pronounced undulating grain. Ideal for kitchenware, chopping boards, and outdoor/indoor tables.

### B. Metal Fabrication & Fusion
* Hand-forged wrought iron with electro-static powder coating (matte black, oil-rubbed bronze, antique brass).
* Jaali (lattice) fretwork: Retaining traditional Rajasthani architectural motifs while framing them in minimalist, modern silhouettes.

### C. Trending Finishes in US & EU (2025–2027)
1. **Raw Natural & Light Honey**: Minimal lacquer, organic matte wax finish highlighting natural grain.
2. **Yakisugi / Charred Charcoal**: Blackened wood paired with natural metal accents.
3. **Warm Walnut & Antique Distressed**: Timeless aesthetic favored by US catalogue brands like Pottery Barn, Restoration Hardware, and West Elm.

## 4. International Regulatory & Export Compliance Guide
To successfully export to the US and EU without customs delays or penalties, Arawali Arts must integrate these standards:

1. **VRIKSH Timber Legality Verification Scheme**:
   - Run by the Export Promotion Council for Handicrafts (EPCH).
   - Serves as proof of legal timber origin, satisfying the **US Lacey Act** and European regulations for Dalbergia sissoo (Sheesham).
2. **EUDR (European Union Deforestation Regulation)**:
   - Mandatory geolocation and traceability proving timber is not from deforested land.
3. **Moisture Control & Kiln Seasoning**:
   - Timber must be dried to **8% – 12% moisture content** in kiln drying chambers. This prevents cracking, warping, and mold during ocean container transit across varying temperature zones.
4. **Phytosanitary Certification & Fumigation**:
   - Methyl Bromide or Heat Treatment (ISPM 15) for wood and solid packaging materials to ensure zero pest/wood-borer infestation.
5. **ISTA-3A Drop-Test Packaging Standards**:
   - 5-ply or 7-ply corrugated outer cartons, corner protectors, high-density EPE foam wrapping, and drop-tested master cartons (crucial for fragile iron/wood fusion sconces).
