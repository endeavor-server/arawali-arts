# Competitor Website Benchmark & UX Audit — Jodhpur Furniture Exporters

## 1. Executive Summary
This audit benchmarks leading Jodhpur export houses (**Bothra International**, **Latiyal Handicrafts**, **Prince Art Export**, **Aztec Shiva**) against modern global B2B procurement expectations in the United States and Europe.

Traditional Jodhpur exporter websites suffer from critical digital friction points that lose wholesale buyers. Arawali Arts' digital architecture addresses every single weakness to establish immediate authority, frictionless container planning, and higher conversion rates.

---

## 2. In-Depth Competitor Website Analysis

### A. Bothra International (`bothrainternational.com`)
* **Visual Presentation**: Traditional mid-2000s catalog layout. High volume of thumbnail photos, but lacks contextual lifestyle staging.
* **Key Friction Points**:
  * Static "brochureware" architecture with slow-loading image galleries.
  * No interactive container volume or CBM calculation; buyers are forced to manually request spreadsheets.
  * Generic contact form with no SKU auto-population.
  * Hidden or difficult-to-download specifications.

### B. Latiyal Handicrafts (`latiyal.in`)
* **Visual Presentation**: Clean, rustic visual tone highlighting reclaimed wood textures and cast-iron machinery. Good integration of trade fair banners (High Point Market & Las Vegas Market).
* **Key Friction Points**:
  * Website functions strictly as a portfolio supporting physical trade shows; zero self-serve B2B quoting.
  * No retail customer validation (e.g., consumer review proofs, Amazon velocity data).
  * Missing interactive filtering for buyers seeking specific wood species (Sheesham vs Mango vs Acacia).

### C. Prince Art Export (`princeartexporter.com`)
* **Visual Presentation**: Focus on vintage reproductions, heavy carved pillars, and bone inlay.
* **Key Friction Points**:
  * Cluttered category trees making navigation difficult for western sourcing agents.
  * Minimal technical information regarding wood moisture content (EMC), VRIKSH certifications, or EUDR geolocation readiness.
